Privacy policy for Still • Positive Affirmations.

Version 1.0.0. Effective 3 November 2026.

The short version

Still is published by sohaibak6. The extension works offline and keeps your choices on your device. It does not send your favorites or preferences to the publisher or to an external service. It has no account system, advertisements, or analytics.

What stays on your device

Still uses Chrome's local extension storage to remember your saved affirmation identifiers, selected topic, light or dark appearance, saved collection view, and the seed and position of your shuffled collection. These preferences let you reopen the popup where you left it.

Still does not use Chrome's storage sync API or provide its own cloud synchronization. The affirmation library, interface, icons, and font are bundled with the extension.

What the extension does not collect

Still does not collect names, email addresses, passwords, payment information, location, browsing history, or the contents of websites. It does not create logs of clicks, keystrokes, or browsing activity. It has no content scripts and does not read other tabs.

The extension does not make network requests. Its only browser permission is storage, used for the local preferences described above.

How your choices are used

Your choices are used only to display and organize affirmations and preserve the appearance and collection position you select. The publisher does not receive, sell, share, or use these local preferences for advertising.

Keeping or removing your choices

Local preferences remain in the browser profile while the extension is installed. You can remove saved thoughts with the heart control. Uninstalling Still removes its extension storage. Still does not maintain a server copy that needs a separate deletion request.

Your browser or operating system may have its own profile backup behavior, which Still does not operate.

If you contact support

If you email sohaibk1001@gmail.com, the publisher receives the address and message you choose to send. These messages are handled in the support mailbox to answer your request. Email is processed by the email provider under its own policies and is separate from the extension's local storage.

You can use the same address to ask about a support message or request its deletion. Removing the extension does not remove an email you previously sent.

This page and future changes

This page has no analytics or cookies added by Still. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub privacy statement. The extension itself does not contact this website.

This policy describes version 1.0.0. If a future release changes how information is handled, the policy and store disclosures will be updated to describe that release.
