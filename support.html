Open Still

Choose Chrome's Extensions menu, find Still, and pin it. Click the Still icon in the toolbar to open your affirmation popup.

Turn to another thought

Click Next affirmation or press Space. The right arrow moves forward and the left arrow returns to the previous thought. Keyboard navigation pauses while the topic picker is open.

Keep a favorite

Tap the heart beneath a thought to save it. Open the bookmark control at the top to visit your saved collection. Tap the bookmark again to return to the main library.

Change topics or appearance

Choose A little of everything to see the available topics. The sun and moon control switches between light and dark. Still also respects your system's reduced motion preference.

Something is not working

Close and reopen the popup first. If the problem remains, email the support address with your Chrome version, Still version, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot is useful if you are comfortable sharing one.

Your local favorites and preferences are not available to the publisher. Uninstalling the extension removes them, so keep any words you want before reinstalling.

Accounts and purchases

Version 1.0.0 is free and does not require an account or subscription. Any future paid features will be described separately before purchase.
